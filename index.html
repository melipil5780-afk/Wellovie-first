Common questions
Do I need to cancel my current tools to use Wellovie?
No. Wellovie is designed to sit alongside your existing stack. Keep your EHR, your scheduler, your telehealth platform. We handle everything Wellovie is built for — the rest stays exactly as-is.
I already have a Psychology Today listing. Why do I need this?
Psychology Today puts you in a list. Wellovie builds your full presence — a professional site that sounds like you, a verified directory with real specialty filters, and tools that make clients want to reach out. It's the difference between being listed and being found.
What if I'm not good at writing my own copy?
That's exactly what Wellovie solves. You answer questions about your work; Wellovie writes the copy. Built by a clinician who knows how therapists talk about their practice — not a generic AI template.
How is founding member pricing different?
The first 100 therapists who join get a locked-in rate for as long as they're subscribed — meaning you'll never pay full price even as Wellovie grows. It's our way of building with the people who believed in it first.
